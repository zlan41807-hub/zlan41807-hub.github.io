Clear lanes

Who leads what.

A good project has one leader per lane. We own the technical system; you own the project — and we do not blur the line to win work.

JFA coordinates

The technical building system.

  • Scheme design and documentation
  • Structural design coordination and loads
  • Specification, openings and finishes schedules
  • Approval documentation, prepared to be questioned
  • Technical questions, start to finish

You lead

The project itself.

  • Land, ownership and title
  • Approvals, permits and statutory fees
  • Site preparation and ground works
  • Contractor and trade engagement
  • Utility connections and construction works

Both a self-build and a fully contracted build fit this model — the split above does not change, only who signs which contract.
