Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Ask us directly — one of the four teams will answer.

What can I send?
A phone photo of a hand sketch is enough, and a plan from another designer works too. PDF, JPG and PNG all open. If all you have is a description, send that and we will tell you whether it is enough to start.
Do I need any software?
No. We build and keep the model. What you receive are ordinary files, so nothing needs installing on your side.
Is this a redesign?
No. We draw the idea you bring, and we tell you where it has to change to work. A different concept, a replacement layout or a substantial change is a separate design commission, quoted before any work starts.
What if my idea cannot be built?
We say so before you spend money on drawings, and we tell you which constraint stops it — usually the site, the planning rules or the structure. Knowing that early is worth something on its own.
What counts as one round of revision?
One consolidated list of changes, sent together, staying inside the scheme you have already approved. Two rounds are included. Anything after that is handled as a revision.
What do I actually end up owning?
The scheme drawings, the rendered views and the cost note, as files, by email. They are yours to keep and to hand to a contractor or an approval consultant.
Does this mean my building is approved or engineered?
No. This stage checks whether the idea works as a design. Statutory approvals and site-specific structural design are separate, and are quoted by whoever carries them out.
How current is the cost figure?
Every note states the date it was prepared, the basis it rests on and what is excluded. It is an order-of-magnitude figure for decisions, not a fixed price from a contractor.
