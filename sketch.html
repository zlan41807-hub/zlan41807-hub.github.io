What can I send?我可以发什么？
A phone photo of a hand sketch is enough, and a plan from another designer works too. PDF, JPG and PNG all open. If all you have is a description, send that and we will tell you whether it is enough to start.手机拍的手绘草图就够，别家设计师的平面也可以。PDF、JPG、PNG 都能打开。如果只有文字描述，也可以先发过来，我们会告诉你够不够开始。
Do I need any software?我需要装什么软件吗？
No. We build and keep the model. What you receive are ordinary files, so nothing needs installing on your side.不需要。模型由我们建立和保管。你收到的都是普通文件，你那边不用装任何东西。
Is this a redesign?这算是重新设计吗？
No. We draw the idea you bring, and we tell you where it has to change to work. A different concept, a replacement layout or a substantial change is a separate design commission, quoted before any work starts.不是。我们画的是你带来的想法，并告诉你哪些地方必须调整才能成立。换概念、换布局或大改动属于另一个设计委托，开工前单独报价。
What if my idea cannot be built?如果我的想法建不了呢？
We say so before you spend money on drawings, and we tell you which constraint stops it — usually the site, the planning rules or the structure. Knowing that early is worth something on its own.我们会在你为图纸花钱之前就说清，并告诉你是哪个条件卡住了 —— 通常是场地、报建规则或结构。早一点知道这件事本身就有价值。
What counts as one round of revision?一轮修订是怎么算的？
One consolidated list of changes, sent together, staying inside the scheme you have already approved. Two rounds are included. Anything after that is handled as a revision.一次性把所有修改意见列齐发来，并且限定在已确认的方案范围内。含两轮。此后的改动按修订处理。
What do I actually end up owning?最后我拿到的是什么？
The scheme drawings, the rendered views and the cost note, as files, by email. They are yours to keep and to hand to a contractor or an approval consultant.方案图纸、效果图和造价说明，以文件形式邮件发给你。文件归你保管，可以直接交给施工方或报建顾问。
Does this mean my building is approved or engineered?这等于报建通过或结构算过了吗？
No. This stage checks whether the idea works as a design. Statutory approvals and site-specific structural design are separate, and are quoted by whoever carries them out.不等于。这个阶段只判断想法作为设计是否成立。报建审批和针对场地的结构设计是另外的事，由实际承担的一方报价。
How current is the cost figure?造价数字有多新？
Every note states the date it was prepared, the basis it rests on and what is excluded. It is an order-of-magnitude figure for decisions, not a fixed price from a contractor.每一份说明都会写明编制日期、依据和未包含的内容。它是用于决策的量级数字，不是施工方的固定报价。
