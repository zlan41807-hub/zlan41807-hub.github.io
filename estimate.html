What "reviewed by a person" means「人工复核」具体指什么

A member of our team reads the design, the pricing basis and what is still unknown before the report is issued, and holds it back if something needs fixing first. It gives you a clearer basis for a decision — not a promise of an all-in construction price. It is not checked or approved by a cost consultant, engineer, authority or lender, and we are not your contractor or certifier. 在报告签发前，我们的成员会审阅方案、计价依据和仍未明确的部分；发现问题会先退回。它让你有一个更清晰的决策依据 —— 而不是承诺一个全包工程总价。它未经造价咨询、结构工程师、主管部门或贷款方审核或批准，我们也不是你的施工方或认证方。

Do I need a finished design first?必须先有完整方案吗？

Must I buy the estimate before the drawings?必须先买估算，才能买图纸包吗？
No. The Preliminary Package includes the Detailed Estimate, prepared from the version you submit. Buying it first is optional.不需要。图纸前置包本身就包含详细估算，按你提交的版本编制。先单独买只是可选项。
Why buy the estimate on its own at all?那为什么要单独买估算？
When you want to check the cost and its basis before you decide whether to spend on preliminary documents at all. Some projects stop or change direction at that point — and that is useful.当你想在决定是否投入前置文件之前，先把费用及其依据核查清楚。有些项目就是在这一步停住或调整方向的 —— 这本身就是价值。

What happens if I change the design afterwards?之后改方案怎么办？
What we issue relates to the version you submitted; later edits do not change it. An updated estimate for a changed scheme or site is a revision (US$79). A revised drawing set follows our terms and is not an automatic free update.我们签发的只对应你提交的那一版，后续改动不会改变它。方案或场地变更后更新估算按修订处理（US$79）；图纸集的修订按我们的条款执行，不属于自动免费更新。
How current is the price basis?价格基准有多新？
Every report states the date of the price basis it used, and is valid 30 days from issue. Beyond that, material and labour prices may have moved — ask us to reissue before you rely on the figures.每份报告都会写明所用价格基准的日期，并自签发起 30 天内有效。超过之后，材料与人工价格可能已经变动 —— 依赖这些数字之前，让我们重新签发。
