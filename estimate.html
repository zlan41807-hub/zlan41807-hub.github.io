Three questions the review answers 这份复核回答的三件事

A number on its own is not much use. What matters is what it rests on, what it excludes, and what nobody has checked yet. 单独一个数字没有意义。真正有价值的是：它建立在什么之上、排除了什么、以及还有什么是没人核实过的。

01

What will it cost — and what is that based on?要花多少钱，依据是什么？

The estimated cost to build the design you submitted, broken down by floor area, with every assumption stated beside the figure so you can see what would move it: structure system, design loads and zoning, and the date of the price basis used. 你提交方案的建造成本估算，按建筑面积分解；每一个假设都写在数字旁边，让你看得见"什么会改变这个数字"：结构体系、设计荷载与设防分区、以及所用价格基准的日期。

02

What do I still have to arrange and budget for?还有哪些要我另行安排和预算？

The estimate covers what we price. It lists — and deliberately does not put a number on — the scope your contractor and consultants price. That list is often the most useful page in the report, because it is where budgets actually break. 估算只覆盖我们计价的部分。报告会列出 —— 并且故意不报价 —— 由施工方和顾问单位计价的范围。这一页往往是最有用的一页，因为预算通常就是在这里失控的。

03

What do we know, and what still needs checking?哪些已知，哪些还必须核实？

Every fact carries its source. What public records show, what you told us, and what still needs a professional are kept strictly apart — an answer you give is never presented as a verified site condition. 每一条信息都标明来源。公开资料显示的、你告诉我们的、以及必须由专业人员核实的，严格分开 —— 你提供的信息绝不会被写成"已核实的场地条件"。

Questions 常见问题

Do I need a finished design first?必须先有完整方案吗？
Yes — a chosen or created design is the basis for both products. You do not need to own the land first, and you do not need to have drawn anything yourself: picking one of our published designs counts as your design.是 —— 两份产品都基于一个确定的方案。你不需要先拿到地，也不需要自己画：选用我们的现成设计就算作"你的方案"。
Must I buy the estimate before the drawings?必须先买估算，才能买图纸包吗？
No. The Preliminary Package includes the Detailed Estimate, prepared from the version you submit. Buying it first is optional.不需要。图纸前置包本身就包含详细估算，按你提交的版本编制。先单独买只是可选项。
If I already bought the estimate, do I pay twice?已经买过估算，再买图纸包会重复付费吗？
The package costs the same either way — the estimate fee is not deducted. Instead we complete the estimate already in progress, or issue an updated one for the design you submit, at no further charge, so you hold one current report rather than two.两种情况包价相同 —— 估算费用不会折抵。但我们会把已经在做的估算做完，或就你提交的方案免费更新一版，让你手上只有一份最新报告，而不是两份。
Is this a fixed construction price, or engineering?这是固定总价或结构设计吗？
No. The Detailed Estimate is an indicative cost estimate — not a supply quotation, not a fixed construction price and not an engineering certificate. Preliminary documents are for early project and planning discussions, not for construction or certification. Site-specific structural design comes later and the formal quotation follows it.不是。详细估算是指示性造价估算 —— 不是供货报价、不是固定工程总价、也不是结构设计成果。前置图纸用于前期方案与报建洽谈，不能用于施工或认证。针对场地的结构计算在此之后，正式报价再随后。
What does "reviewed by a person" actually mean?"人工复核"具体指什么？
A member of our team reads the design, the pricing basis and what is still unknown before the report is issued, and holds it back if something needs fixing first. It gives you a clearer basis for a decision — it is not checked or approved by a cost consultant, engineer, authority or lender, and we are not your contractor or certifier.在报告签发前，我们的成员会审阅方案、计价依据和仍未明确的部分；如果发现问题会先退回不签发。它让你有一个更清晰的决策依据 —— 但它未经造价咨询、结构工程师、主管部门或贷款方审核或批准，我们也不是你的施工方或认证方。
Can I order before I have the land?还没拿到地可以下单吗？
For the Detailed Estimate, yes — without a site the report covers what to look for when you choose one and leaves out the site pages. A Preliminary Package is drawn for the building on a nominated site, so you will need that address to place it.估算可以 —— 没有场地时，报告会写"选地时该看什么"，并省略场地页。图纸前置包必须落在指定场地上，所以你需要有具体地址才能完成摆放。
What happens if I change the design afterwards?之后改方案怎么办？
What we issue relates to the version you submitted; later edits do not change it. An updated estimate for a changed scheme or site is a revision ($99); a revised drawing set follows our terms and is not an automatic free update.我们签发的只对应你提交的那一版，后续改动不会改变它。方案或场地变更后更新估算按修订处理（$99）；图纸集的修订按我们的条款执行，不属于自动免费更新。
How current is the price basis?价格基准有多新？
Every report states the date of the price basis it used, and is valid 30 days from issue. Beyond that, material and labour prices may have moved — ask us to reissue before you rely on the figures.每份报告都会写明所用价格基准的日期，并自签发之日起 30 天内有效。超过之后，材料与人工价格可能已经变动 —— 依赖这些数字之前，让我们重新签发。
