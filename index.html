About Us 关于我们

Hangzhou Jinfang Architectural Design Co., Ltd. is a full-chain building solution provider dedicated to delivering integrated architectural excellence 杭州谨防建筑设计有限公司是一家专注于建筑全产业链的一站式解决方案提供商

We bring together top-tier talent across every architectural discipline — from master planning and schematic design to construction detailing and project management — covering every stage of the building lifecycle. Whether it's a mixed-use complex, luxury hotel, healthcare facility, or commercial space, we deliver professional, efficient solutions. 我们汇聚了建筑行业各领域的顶尖人才，从前期规划、方案设计到施工图深化、项目管理，覆盖建筑全生命周期的每一个环节。无论是城市综合体、高端酒店、医疗建筑还是商业空间，我们都能提供专业、高效的解决方案。

Since our founding, we have completed over 200 projects across more than 20 countries and regions. We believe: Every building is a promise for the future. 自成立以来，我们已完成超过200个项目，业务覆盖20多个国家和地区。我们坚信：每一座建筑，都是对未来的承诺。

Core Services 核心业务

Eight core business segments covering the full building lifecycle — from concept to completion 覆盖建筑全产业链的八大核心业务板块，从创意到落地一站式交付

Architectural Design建筑设计

From concept to construction documents — mixed-use, hospitality, office, and cultural projects. 从概念方案到施工图设计，涵盖城市综合体、酒店、办公、文化建筑等多种类型。

Interior Design室内设计

Commercial, workplace, and luxury residential interiors that blend function with human experience. 商业空间、办公环境与高端住宅室内设计，融合功能美学与人性化体验。

Landscape Design景观设计

Urban public spaces, parks, commercial landscapes, and residential communities. 城市公共空间、公园绿地、商业景观与居住区景观的全方位设计服务。

Structural Engineering结构工程

Advanced structural analysis and design — super-tall, long-span, and complex geometries. 高难度结构分析与设计，涵盖超高层、大跨度、复杂异形结构等创新方案。

BIM ConsultingBIM咨询

Multi-discipline BIM, clash detection, construction simulation, and digital delivery. 全专业BIM建模、碰撞检测、施工模拟与数字化交付，提升项目协作效率。

Project Management工程管理

End-to-end project management — budget, schedule, and quality control. 全流程项目管理服务，从预算控制、进度管理到质量监督，确保项目高效交付。

Furnishing & Decoration家具软装

Integrated furnishing solutions — custom furniture, curtains, lighting, and accessories that harmonize with architectural space. 整体软装方案设计、定制家具选配、窗帘布艺、灯具饰品一站式配置，打造与建筑完美融合的空间美学。

Door & Window Systems门窗系统

Full range of door & window systems — thermal break, curtain wall, smart windows — balancing energy efficiency, safety, and aesthetics. 断桥铝门窗、系统门窗、幕墙门窗、智能门窗系统等全系列设计与供应，兼顾节能、安全与美学。
